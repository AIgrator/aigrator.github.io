Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Overview

Video Converter works offline. No personal data is stored on our servers. No login or registration required.

Your privacy is our top priority. Video Converter processes all files locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements. You can permanently remove ads with a one-time purchase inside the app.

  • Ads are provided by third-party advertising networks
  • These networks may collect data according to their own privacy policies
  • After purchasing ad removal, no advertisements are displayed and no ad-related data is collected

Permissions We Use

Video Converter requires the following permissions to function properly:

PermissionPurpose
READ_MEDIA_VIDEOAccess video files on your device (Android 13+)
READ_MEDIA_AUDIOAccess audio files on your device (Android 13+)
FOREGROUND_SERVICEAllow background video conversion with a persistent notification
WAKE_LOCKKeep the device awake during long conversions
INTERNETRequired for displaying advertisements

Local File Processing

All video and audio conversion is performed locally on your device using FFmpeg. Your files:

  • Never leave your device
  • Are never uploaded to any server
  • Are never shared with third parties
  • Are processed entirely offline

Converted files are saved to your device's Downloads folder (VideoConverter/).

Children's Privacy

Video Converter does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, this policy applies to all users regardless of age.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach or unauthorized access. All processing happens on your device, under your control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
