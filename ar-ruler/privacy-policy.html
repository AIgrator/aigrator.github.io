Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

AR Ruler works entirely offline. No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted. No login or registration required.

Your privacy is our top priority. All measurement processing happens locally on your device. We do not have servers, do not collect data, and do not track your activity.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

  • No registration or account required
  • No email addresses, names, or phone numbers collected
  • No device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, etc.) collected
  • No location tracking
  • No browsing history monitoring
  • All measurement data stays on your device
  • Your camera feed is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere

Local Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Settings: Your unit preferences, tolerance, and theme choices
  • Measurements: Saved measurement history and points

This data never leaves your device and is only used to provide a personalized experience.

Camera and AR

The app uses your device's camera exclusively for augmented reality measurement. The camera feed is:

  • Processed in real-time on your device
  • Never recorded or stored
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Never shared with third parties

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services — for ARCore (augmented reality)

These services have their own privacy policies. Google's privacy policy can be found at policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

The app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera — Required for AR measurement. Feed is processed in real-time and never stored.
  • Internet — Required only for downloading the AI model (SAM2).

The app does not require access to your files, photos, contacts, or location.

Children's Privacy

AR Ruler: Tape & Auto Outline does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, this policy applies to all users regardless of age.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach. All processing happens on your device under your control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
